# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-22

Meet Puppet is designed to process your recordings on-device.

## What the App Does On-Device
When you record a meeting, the App may create and store the following artifacts on your device:
- Audio recording file
- Transcript text
- Summary text
- Session-scoped local retrieval database (for Q&A)

## Data Sharing
By default, your audio and text artifacts remain on your device.

The App only sends data off-device if you explicitly choose to share it using the system Share sheet (for example, sharing a summary as a file). The content you share and the destination are controlled by you.

## Deleting Your Data
You can delete any saved meeting session from the Summaries/Library screen. Deleting a session removes its audio, transcript, summary, and local Q&A database from your device. This action cannot be undone.

## Analytics and Tracking
The App does not intentionally collect analytics or track you across apps.

## Changes
This policy may be updated over time. Updates will be reflected in this document within the App.
